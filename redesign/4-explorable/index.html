Pop quiz before we start!

True or false?

“We only use 10% of our brains.”

✓It’s false. The 10% idea is a myth.

Brain imaging shows activity right across the brain. There’s no idle 90% waiting to be unlocked.

Treta once heard this from a 12-year-old in her class, who added that only Einstein ever used all of his. The next day she brought in a neuroscientist’s article on it, retitled “You Can Be Smarter Than Albert Einstein.”

Her take: our brains are amazing (blow a kiss to yours!).
