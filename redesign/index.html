Eight ideas for a new website

Same person, same facts, eight different moods. Click into any of them and scroll all the way down; they work on a phone too. Then pick a favourite by its number, or mix and match ("the hero from 3 with the colours of 6").

Modern

Inspired by the sites of learning designers and researchers

1

Studio

A confident learning-design portfolio: big friendly headline, portrait with badges, case-study cards with real outcomes, a clear "say hello" ending.

Inspired by amandalxd.com and sabrinaglxd.com
2

Index

A researcher's home page: everything on one screen in side-by-side columns (work, projects, writing, learning) set in violet on blush.

Inspired by andymatuschak.org
3

Editorial

Calm and warm in the dark: a mixed-weight serif headline, her essays as illustrated cards, classroom notes, and work as case files.

Inspired by maggieappleton.com
4

Explorable

The site is a little lesson about Treta: it opens with a pop quiz on the 10%-brain myth, has tap-to-reveal facts and a clickable timeline, and ends with a quiz.

Inspired by ncase.me and beth-myers.com

Paper & print

Warm, tactile, made-by-hand feel

5

The Notebook

A teacher's dot-grid notebook: ballpoint margin notes, a taped polaroid, sticky-note numbers, pinned project cards. The most playful and personal.
6

The Gazette

A warm newsprint broadsheet, a wink at her journalism diploma. Halftone portrait, columns, drop caps, her essays on the opinion page.
7

Cut Paper

Layered construction-paper collage in terracotta, mustard and sage, a nod to her art-integrated STEM teaching. Bold and joyful.
8

A Letter

Quiet letterpress stationery. The homepage reads as a first-person letter from Treta, signed by hand, with an envelope for contact.
All eight use only facts from Treta's résumé, her old site and her Medium essays. Lines written in her voice are drafts for her to edit, and any photo, colour or section can be swapped between versions.
