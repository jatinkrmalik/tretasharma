A few things you should know

I started in classrooms, moved into coaching teachers, and then into building learning at scale. The thread through all of it: fostering diversity, equity and inclusion, one lesson at a time.

I went back to school

…at the brain age of 32!

My family joked that I’d enrolled in a Master’s at the age of raising kids. I wanted to stop only consuming knowledge and become a better distributor of it, so off I went to Northwestern for Learning Sciences.

Where I’ve worked

From the classroom to the platform

Each step took me a little further from one classroom, and closer to the systems that shape thousands of them.

Feb 2022 – Apr 2023

LEAD Schools · EdTech unicorn, India

Curriculum Excellence Analyst

Where pedagogy met product: one curriculum, millions of learners, five education boards.

  • Built digital learning experiences for 1.2M+ learners in 3,000+ schools, steered by learning analytics.
  • Ran 50+ user-research sessions and used the TPACK framework to shape how technology showed up in classrooms and in the interface.
  • Worked hand-in-hand with product and engineering to merge the requirements of 5 education boards into one learning platform.
  • Coached teachers through structured debriefs; partner schools saw a 20% lift in student performance.
May 2019 – Jan 2022

Shiv Nadar Foundation · HCL Technologies

Learning Experience Designer & Faculty

Where art and science shared a desk.

  • Designed art-integrated STEM programs for 450+ learners across grade levels.
  • Built gamified assessments around Bloom’s taxonomy and Depth of Knowledge.
  • Ran design-thinking workshops where students studied problems in their own communities and prototyped fixes, tech and otherwise.
  • Trained fellow faculty in technology-enhanced teaching, with curriculum shaped by India’s National Education Policy.
2017 – 2019

Teach For India

Fellow, then Fellow Advisor

Where it all started: a classroom of first-generation learners.

  • Taught 120+ first-generation learners in low-income schools.
  • Trained 100+ incoming fellows at the residential institute and coached 53 of them through the year.
  • Observed classrooms and ran 1:1 debriefs as an instructional coach.
  • Sat on a School Management Committee, working with local MLAs to win resources for the school.
